## About the journal club

Interested in pursuing graduate studies in astronomy? Join a small group of students to read, present and critically discuss scientific papers, explore research methods, question findings, and talk with international researchers and paper authors.

## A typical session

1. **Updates.** We catch up on news, such as a conversation someone had with the author of a paper we discussed.
2. **Presentation.** One member presents that day's paper, in 20 minutes at most.
3. **Discussion.** We spend several minutes on the paper: asking questions, and suggesting questions the presenter can put to the author when they get the chance.
4. **Wrap-up.** We sum up, confirm who presents next, and go over the meetings coming up that week.

## Requirements

- Interest in astronomical research.
- Commitment to weekly attendance, preparation and active participation.
